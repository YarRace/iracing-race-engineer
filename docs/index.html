What changed lately

  • Tyre Tool: what the tread edges say, and what they were saying wrong2026-09-01

    The tread tells you two things without any guesswork. A hot inner edge means there is more negative camber than the corner is using. A hot middle means the tyre is crowned, which is pressure. Both are physics and neither depends on the car.

  • Setup optimiser: say what the car does, get what to turn2026-09-01

    Two dropdowns — where in the corner, and what the car does — and you get an ordered list of what to change in the garage, each with the reason it works.

  • Sectors: the mistake you can drive around, and the one you cannot2026-09-01

    A delta to your best lap is one number, and it moves the whole way round. It cannot tell you whether you lost the time in the first sector or clawed it back in the third. The only way to find out was to finish, climb out of the car and open a tab — which, on a long practice, nobody does.

Why it works this way

Everything in one window

Telemetry, strategy, lap history and analysis — instead of four apps with four subscriptions.

An overlay of your own

Every widget is tuned on its own: colour, size and font of each number. Wheel buttons map to any action.

Nothing gets lost

Every lap lands on disk with its telemetry. History survives between sessions, so progress shows across dates, not one drive.

No smoothing

Raw values at a high refresh rate. Smoothing hides exactly what you open the numbers for — the scatter and the outliers.

Analysis in plain words

After a stint the model explains what the car was doing and suggests setup changes, with the reasoning attached.

Works offline

It all runs locally. No keys to anyone else's service, and no data leaves the machine.

Your data, and who owns this

Everything stays on your machine. Laps, telemetry, track maps and settings are written next to the program and go nowhere else — no account, no upload, no analytics. Two features reach the network because they cannot work otherwise, and only once you set them up: Garage 61 for other drivers' laps, with your own token, and the racing news feed. Neither sends anything about you.
